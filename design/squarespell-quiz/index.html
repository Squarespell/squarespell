Before you build

Straight answers.

Is Squarespell Quiz only for Squarespace?

The current product has a Squarespace connection flow and three embed modes. Inline, popup and floating tab can also be placed on websites that support the required custom code. Native one-click installation is not available on every platform, so each platform page must explain its real installation method.

What can the AI generate?

Five modes exist in the current engine: lead quiz, price calculator, service recommender, client qualifier and segmentation quiz. The generator reads website content and brand signals, then drafts questions, scoring and outcomes for review.

How many templates are included?

The current template library contains 16 templates across photography, food, fitness, ecommerce, weddings, coaching, interior design, beauty, creators, real estate, travel and nonprofits. Three include video questions.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

The trial uses Pro features. The current product copy says users choose Core, Pro or Business to continue. The finished relaunch must keep already collected leads available and explain any pause in new lead capture before checkout.

Is this the same as the one-time Squarespell plugin?

No. The one-time plugin remains a separate marketplace product at squarespell.com. Squarespell Quiz is the subscription application. Existing plugin customers and marketplace pages must remain untouched.
